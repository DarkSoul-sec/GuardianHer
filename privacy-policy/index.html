1. Overview

GuardianHer is a personal safety application for Android devices designed to help you alert trusted people in emergency situations. The app is designed with privacy as a core principle - most data is stored locally on your device.

Application ID: com.GuardianHer.app
Version: 2.0.0

This policy applies to the GuardianHer Android application distributed through Google Play Store.

3. Data We Access and Process

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Trusted Contacts: Names and phone numbers of emergency contacts you manually add. Stored locally on your device.
  • App Settings: Your preferences for safety features (Shake SOS, check-in reminders, etc.). Stored locally.
  • Voice Recordings: Audio notes you explicitly record using the Voice Recordings feature. Stored locally as files on your device.
  • Vehicle Information: Optional vehicle details you enter for journey tracking (registration, driver name, booking reference). Stored only in memory during the journey, never persisted long-term.

3.2 Information Accessed Automatically

  • Location Data: Accessed only when you explicitly trigger location capture (activating SOS, confirming a safety check-in, or manually sharing location). Not collected in the background continuously. No background location access is requested.
  • Device Information: Basic device state for functionality (battery status for emergency messages, network connectivity state). Not collected for tracking or analytics.

4. Emergency Features

4.1 Emergency SMS

When you activate SOS, GuardianHer automatically sends emergency SMS messages from your device to your selected emergency contacts if the required SMS permission is granted. Messages may include your current or last known location, when available, and emergency details. SMS is transmitted through your mobile carrier; standard messaging charges may apply.

4.2 Location Sharing

Location is captured on demand for SOS, safety check-ins, or when you choose to share it. During SOS, an available location may be included in emergency SMS messages to your selected contacts. For other sharing, you choose whether to open an SMS or email composer, or use the Android share sheet. Map links may be included in messages.

4.3 Other Emergency Features

  • Shake SOS: Optional feature that detects deliberate shakes to trigger SOS. Runs as a foreground service only when enabled by you.
  • Quick Settings Tile & Widget: Launchers that open the SOS flow when tapped. No data collection.
  • Fake Call: Simulated feature with no real calls or microphone recording.
  • Safety Check-Ins: Scheduled reminders that you manually confirm. Location captured only upon confirmation.

5. Permissions

GuardianHer requests only the permissions necessary for its features:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: For one-time location capture during emergencies or check-ins
  • SEND_SMS: Allows GuardianHer to send SOS emergency SMS messages to your selected contacts after you activate SOS. If permission is denied, automatic SOS SMS is unavailable; other user-initiated sharing options are separate.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Only for voice notes you explicitly record
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: For SOS alerts and safety reminders
  • VIBRATE: For emergency alerts
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE variants: For Shake SOS detection and emergency services while app is in background/locked state
  • WAKE_LOCK: To ensure emergency SMS is sent reliably
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Optional, only for Shake SOS to launch from locked screen
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore user-enabled features after device reboot

You can deny any permission and the related feature will be unavailable with a clear explanation. No permissions are requested at app startup.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Most data (contacts, settings, safety records) is stored locally on your device.
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive data like trusted contacts is stored using Android Keystore-backed secure storage (via flutter_secure_storage).
  • Voice Recordings: Stored as audio files in the app's private documents directory, accessible only to the app.
  • Safety Incidents: Emergency event logs are stored locally in app preferences for your review.
  • No Cloud Sync: Data is not synced to cloud services.
  • App Isolation: Other apps cannot access GuardianHer's private data.

While we use platform security features, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We implement reasonable security measures using Android's built-in security mechanisms.

7. Data Sharing

GuardianHer does not send your personal data to GuardianHer-operated servers or to analytics and advertising providers. Data leaves your device only through the sharing features you use.

The only data transmissions that occur are:

  • Emergency SMS: When SOS is activated and SMS permission is granted, messages are sent from your device to your selected contacts through your carrier's SMS service
  • Phone Calls: When you explicitly place a call to emergency services or contacts
  • User-Initiated Sharing: When you choose to share location or other content through an SMS or email composer, or the Android share sheet, the chosen recipient or app receives the content
  • Map Links: Messages may contain a Google Maps link for a shared location. If a recipient opens it, Google may process the location and their request under Google's privacy policy

8. Third-Party Services and SDKs

GuardianHer uses the following third-party SDKs for functionality:

  • Flutter Framework & Dart packages: Various open-source packages for UI, state management, local storage, permissions, etc.
  • Android System Services: Uses Android APIs for SMS, location, notifications, etc.
  • Google Maps links: Messages may include maps.google.com links for location sharing - these open in the recipient's map app

No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs are integrated.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Contacts & Settings: Retained until you delete them or clear all data
  • Voice Recordings: Retained until you delete them individually or use "Delete all local data"
  • Safety Incidents: Stored locally until cleared by you
  • In-Memory Data: Some data (like vehicle info during journeys) exists only in memory for the duration of the session

Deletion Methods:

  • Delete individual contacts, voice recordings, or records from within the app
  • Use "Delete all local data" in Settings to wipe contacts, settings, safety incidents, and voice recordings from your device
  • Uninstall the app to remove all app data from your device

10. User Control and Consent

  • Explicit Actions: All emergency features require explicit user action to trigger
  • Permission Control: You can grant or deny permissions at any time in Android settings
  • Feature Opt-In: Features like Shake SOS, device administrator, and check-in reminders are opt-in
  • Data Control: You have full control over your contacts and can delete data at any time
  • No Automatic Tracking: The app does not track your location or send alerts automatically without your action

11. Children's Privacy

GuardianHer is intended for general audiences and is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
